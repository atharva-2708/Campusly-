The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.0.0/),
and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [1.0.0] — 2026-10-04

### Added
- **Core Layout & Navigation:**
  - Responsive desktop sidebar with expandable/collapsible modes.
  - Sticky editorial top navigation with unread alerts dropdown, theme switcher, and dynamic semester badge.
  - Mobile bottom navigation bar and slide-in drawer for smartphone accessibility.
  - 1-Click Demo Role Switcher (`Student` ↔ `Admin`) for seamless evaluation.
- **Academic Resources & Curriculum:**
  - Complete 4-year undergraduate syllabus matrix for 11 engineering and basic science departments.
  - Curated textbook reading room with 35+ volumes, cover artwork, author metadata, and ISBNs.
  - Resource filtering by department, semester, and material type (notes, manuals, question banks).
- **Exam Vault:**
  - 30+ multi-year previous question papers (2022–2025) across End-Sem, Mid-Sem, and Unit Tests.
  - Interactive Solved Exam Paper Modal rendering authentic question sets with marks distribution.
- **Campus Noticeboard & Announcements:**
  - 20 campus notices categorized by Academic, Exam, Placement, Holiday, and Cultural events.
  - Pinned announcement indicators and animated visual badges for urgent circulars.
- **Interactive Campus Masterplan:**
  - Custom SVG vector blueprint with 20 college landmarks, halls, and amenities.
  - Pan and zoom canvas controls with a sliding building detail inspection drawer.
- **Local Amenities & Student Hangouts:**
  - Curated directory of 30 student spots across food, printing, transport, and pharmacies.
  - Walking distance markers and budget tier indicators.
- **Student Life & Freshers' Onboarding:**
  - 12 comprehensive survival guides covering registration, exam survival, and campus etiquette.
  - 10-item onboarding checklist with persistent `localStorage` progress tracking.
- **Live Administrative Office Timings:**
  - Live open/closed calculations based on real computer time and lunch break intervals for 16 offices.
- **Student Utilities:**
  - Coursework deadline tracker with status badges.
  - Personal Study Task Planner with radial completion indicator.
  - Saved Shelf Bookmarks and offline downloads tracking.
- **Global Search:**
  - Keyboard-accessible (`Ctrl+K`) cross-category search querying books, papers, circulars, offices, and campus landmarks.
- **Design System ("The Analog Campus"):**
  - Warm Ivory (`#F6F1E7`), Paper Cream (`#EDE4D4`), Deep Forest (`#183D32`), Terracotta (`#C66B4E`), Academic Mustard (`#D7AA48`), and Sage (`#A8B9A0`).
  - Font pairings: `DM Serif Display`, `DM Sans`, and `DM Mono`.
  - Full Light and Dark theme support.
